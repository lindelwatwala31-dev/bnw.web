Learn Step by Step

Mathematics becomes easier when you break each problem into smaller steps.

Example: Adding Two Numbers

27 + 15 = ?
Step 1: Add the ones: 7 + 5 = 12
Step 2: Write down 2 and carry the 1.
Step 3: Add the tens: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4
Step 4: Therefore, 27 + 15 = 42
